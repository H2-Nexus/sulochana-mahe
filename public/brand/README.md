# Sulochana Mahe — logo files

The mark: a temple-shrine arch (where Kerala murals are painted) holding an oil-lamp flame (the light they were painted to be seen by), with the diamond ornament beneath.

- `sm-mark-*.svg` — mark only (ochre, ink, cream, black). Vector; works at any size.
- `sm-logo-horizontal-*.svg`, `sm-logo-stacked-*.svg` — lockups with wordmark (Instrument Serif) and tagline (DM Mono).
- `sm-avatar-1024.png` — social profile image. `sm-mark-1024.png` — transparent mark.
- `favicon.svg` — browser icon.

Print: the lockup SVGs use live text. Before sending to a printer, open them in Illustrator/Figma with Instrument Serif and DM Mono installed (both free on Google Fonts) and convert text to outlines.

Colours: Ochre #D89A2B · Vermilion #B8401F · Deep red #9E3418 · Leaf green #1E3827 · Lamp black #17100A · Cream #F1E6CC

Clear space: the width of the flame on every side. Minimum size: mark 16 px / 6 mm tall; horizontal lockup 120 px / 30 mm wide.
